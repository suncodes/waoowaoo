Execution requirements:
- Do not fill the entire target duration with narration. Estimate a natural spoken pace and keep the summed segment/beat duration within 85%-92% of targetDurationSec, leaving at least 8% for pauses, transitions, opening/closing, and editorial adjustment.
- Each segment or beat must carry one primary question, claim, or story progression. Do not pack several parallel claims into one unit.
- The first 5-12 seconds must establish one clear question, contrast, or promise without stacking unsupported hooks.
- Narration must be voice-ready, conversational, and concise. Avoid unsupported absolutes such as "the best ever", "all came true", "guaranteed", or global rankings.
- visualPurpose describes the information job of the visual. visualHints must describe a concrete image in one time and place; never ask one still image to perform a news montage, multi-location collage, or several sequential actions.
- Put exact copy only in onScreenText. Do not ask the image model to render book titles, dates, newspaper headlines, or long text.
- Never invent book evidence. Every guide segment requires a sourceAnchor. Do not reduce a guide to plot retelling.
- Guide titles, outline titles, segment titles, and narration must use plain audience-facing language. Do not expose production or marketing jargon such as “hook”, “CTA”, “cold open”, “viral beat”, “conversion”, or “information gain”; rewrite notes like “opening hook + basic-setting explainer” as concrete content such as “ask the opening question” or “introduce the background and basic setting”.
